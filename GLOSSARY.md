# Arcana collection and budget language

Terms for the planned combined collection and budget workflow.

## Language

**Owned lot**:
A quantity of physical cards with the same printing, finish and recorded condition.
_Avoid_: Deck entry, available balance

**Deck demand**:
The copies required by one deck across its selected zones, independent of what the player owns.
_Avoid_: Reserved copies, inventory

**Missing copies**:
The portion of a deck's demand that its ownership matching policy cannot satisfy from the collection.
_Avoid_: Purchased cards

**Deck estimate**:
A dated or saved price estimate for the selected deck copies, including copies already owned.
_Avoid_: Purchase total, appraisal

**Missing-copy estimate**:
A price estimate for copies the player still needs for one deck under its ownership matching policy.
_Avoid_: Guaranteed checkout cost

**Budget**:
A player-set spending target for one deck, compared with a clearly labeled estimate.
_Avoid_: Market price
